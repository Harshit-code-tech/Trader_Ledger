## 🎯 Demo Steps

### Demo 1: Basic Report (No Filters)

**Purpose**: Show baseline - what v1.0 looked like

1. Go to **Reports** tab
2. Click **🔄 Recalculate**
3. **Observe**:
   - Total Profit, Total Loss, Net P/L cards
   - Daily P/L breakdown
   - All trades included

**Expected**:
- Net P/L: Around ₹4,118.40
- Multiple dates shown
- Both TCS and INFY trades included

---

### Demo 2: Filter by Single Stock (Equity Filter)

**Purpose**: Show equity-wise filtering

1. In Reports tab
2. Select **`TCS`** from Equity dropdown
3. Click **🔍 Apply Filters**
4. **Observe**:
   - Summary cards update (only TCS trades)
   - Daily breakdown shows only TCS sell dates
   - Equity-wise summary shows only TCS row

**Expected**:
- Net P/L changes (only TCS profit)
- Fewer dates in breakdown
- INFY completely excluded

**Try Also**:
- Select `INFY` and apply
- Select `All` to see everything again

---

### Demo 3: Filter by Date Range

**Purpose**: Show time period filtering

1. Clear filters first (🔄 Clear Filters)
2. From Date: `2026-01-15`
3. To Date: `2026-01-20`
4. Click **🔍 Apply Filters**
5. **Observe**:
   - Summary cards update
   - Only trades with sell dates in range shown
   - Daily breakdown filtered

**Expected**:
- Sells on 2026-01-15 (TCS) and 2026-01-20 (INFY) included
- Sell on 2026-01-22 excluded
- Net P/L is lower (fewer trades)

**Edge Case Demo**:
1. Note the TCS sell on 2026-01-15
2. That matched with BUY on 2026-01-10 (BEFORE range)
3. **It's still INCLUDED** ✅ (because SELL is in range)

---

### Demo 4: Combined Filters (Equity + Date)

**Purpose**: Show multiple filters working together

1. Equity: `INFY`
2. From Date: `2026-01-15`
3. To Date: `2026-01-20`
4. Click **🔍 Apply Filters**
5. **Observe**:
   - Only INFY trades in date range
   - Very specific, focused analysis

**Expected**:
- Only one sell: INFY on 2026-01-20
- Net P/L around ₹799
- Very targeted report

### Demo 5: View Open Positions

**Purpose**: Show what stocks you're still holding

1. Click **🔄 Clear Filters** to reset
2. Check ✅ **Include Open Positions**
3. **Observe**:
   - New table appears at bottom
   - Shows TCS and INFY
   - Quantity still held
   - Average buy price

**Expected**:
```
Equity | Status | Qty | Avg Price    | Unrealized P/L
TCS    | OPEN   | 20  | ₹3421.00     | ₹0 (no market data)
INFY   | OPEN   | 40  | ₹1507.50     | ₹0 (no market data)
```

**Explanation**:
- TCS: Bought 100, sold 80 → 20 remaining
- INFY: Bought 80, sold 40 → 40 remaining
- Avg price: Weighted average of all BUYs

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "No data" in equity dropdown
**Solution**: Add trades first (Add Trade tab)

### Open positions show zero
**Explanation**: All your shares are sold - this is correct

### Date filter not working
**Check**: Date format must be YYYY-MM-DD

### Numbers don't match
**Verify**: 
1. Check trades in View Records tab
2. Verify quantities and dates
3. Remember: filters affect totals
